Source. City of Chicago 311 Service Requests, request type “Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint”, filtered to Ward 44. Pulled from the Chicago Data Portal on August 26, 2026. This page carries its own copy of the data and its own map, so it needs no keys, no tile server and no live connection.
Where the pin lands. The city geocodes these complaints to the property, not the curb, and rodent baiting happens in the yard and the alley. Across this ward the published coordinate sits a median of metres off the street centreline, and of them land more than 20 metres off, which is roughly the depth of a Chicago lot. Dropping Street View at the raw coordinate therefore lands you behind the building. Each link here is projected back onto the street and aimed at the address instead.
Method. Complaints are grouped into 100-address blocks and drawn at the median coordinate of the reports on that block. The street grid is traced from the reports themselves, so only streets that have generated complaints appear. Closure time runs from created date to closed date, excluding tickets left open past a year. Duplicate flags are kept in, because a duplicate is still a neighbor who called.
Caveat. This maps reports, not rats. A block with organized, persistent neighbors will look worse than a block with the same problem and nobody dialing.
